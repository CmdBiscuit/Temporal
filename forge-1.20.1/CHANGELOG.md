## 1.0.0 (Forge 1.20.1)

First release for Minecraft 1.20.1 on Forge, with everything in Temporal 1.0.0 for NeoForge 1.21.1:

- Upscalers: DLSS, FSR 3, NIS, FSR 1
- Anti-aliasing: DLAA, FSR Native AA, TAA
- Frame generation: DLSS Frame Generation (2x-6x), FSR 3 Frame Generation (2x), with NVIDIA Reflex
- Dynamic resolution, an FPS counter that counts generated frames, hotkeys (F7 / F8)

Works with Embeddium and Oculus (with or without a shader pack).

Differences from the 1.21.1 version:
- The settings screen has no blurred background (Minecraft 1.20.1 menus don't blur).
